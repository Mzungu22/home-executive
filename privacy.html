Home Executive ("the app") is a shared family calendar, meal planner, and to‑do list. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Who runs this app

[YOUR NAME / BUSINESS NAME], contactable at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us there.

What information we collect

  • Account information: the email address you sign up with (or your Apple ID identifier, if you use Sign in with Apple).
  • Family content you enter: calendar events, family member names and colors, meal plans and recipes, to‑do items, shopping list items, public holiday and school term entries, and anything else you type into the app.
  • Optional AI settings: if you choose to connect your own Anthropic or Google Gemini API key for ingredient scanning, that key is stored so the app can use it on your behalf.

We don't collect location data, contacts, or device identifiers, and the app contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

How your information is used

Your account and family content are used solely to provide the app's features to you and the family members you share access with. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see your data

Family content (calendar, meals, to‑dos, shopping list, etc.) is visible to every member of your family group — anyone who created or joined that family using its invite code. It is not visible to other families using the app. Your optional AI API key is private to your own account and is never visible to other family members.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a third‑party cloud service. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Optional AI ingredient scanning

By default, ingredient scanning (pasting recipe text or a photo) is handled entirely on your device with no data sent anywhere. If you choose to connect your own Anthropic or Google Gemini API key, the recipe text or photo you submit for scanning is sent directly from your device to that provider, under your own account with them — not through our servers. Each provider's own privacy terms apply to that data:

  • Anthropic: anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google (Gemini API): ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

Children's information

Accounts are created by an adult family organizer. You may enter the names of children as family members so their events and meals can be color‑coded on the calendar; this is basic profile information entered by the account holder, not collected directly from a child. The app does not knowingly allow children to create their own accounts.

Your choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your family's data directly within the app at any time.
  • Deleting your account: open the menu → Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your sign‑in and personal profile. It does not delete shared family data that other family members still rely on.
  • Removing an AI key: open the menu → Ingredient scanning, and switch back to the free built‑in scanner at any time.

Data retention

We retain your account and family data for as long as your account exists. If every member of a family deletes their account, that family's shared data may remain stored but becomes inaccessible with no way to sign in and view it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above.
